Shakir is the engineering spine of the firm. He sets technical standards for T3NK and embeds with portfolio companies to unblock architecture, performance, and shipping — the work that turns a good thesis into a shipped product.

His craft is leverage: reading a codebase, finding the constraint, and leaving the team faster than he found it. He is allergic to hype and to rewrites that don't ship, and prefers the boring fix that holds.

He also keeps the firm honest about what software can and can't do, and makes sure our diligence survives contact with the repo.
