Kevin keeps the machine quiet. An operations leader who has scaled teams and stripped complex processes down to what actually works, he makes sure the firm runs with precision — so the partners can do capital and code without the back office falling behind either.

His craft is translation: strategy in, systems out. Hiring loops, operating rhythms, the unglamorous pipelines a growing firm and its portfolio both depend on — Kevin builds them once, properly, and they keep running.

Founders rarely notice the work of a great COO, and that is the point.
