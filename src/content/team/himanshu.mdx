Himanshu runs delivery. When a portfolio company takes T3NK engineering help, he is the one who keeps the roadmap, the hiring plan and the reality of the codebase in the same conversation.

He plans in increments, communicates in documents, and treats scope as a decision rather than an apology. Portfolio founders get exactly one thing from him: fewer surprise delays, and an honest answer to "when is it landing" the moment he knows.

Off the plan, he owns the internal tooling that makes delivery visible to everyone, including the founders who'd rather watch demos than Gantt charts.
