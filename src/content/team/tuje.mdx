Tuje runs operations: hiring, finance, and everything portfolio companies need from us that isn't code — capital tables, follow-on paperwork, and the connective tissue between T3NK and its shareholders.

She treats ops like engineering: checklists where humans repeat themselves, automation where software can carry the repetition, and a bias for unblocking over reporting. Founders call her before a crisis; she usually saw it coming a month earlier.

If a portfolio company grows its team by three, a notice has already run through payroll, contracts and onboarding before the founder finishes the thought.
