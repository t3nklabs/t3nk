Aman founded T3NK on the conviction that the right money moves more slowly than the right pull request. He invests at pre-seed and Series A, signs as a director, and then does something most funds don't: joins the repo.

His own engineering work is mostly product and mobile — the first version a portfolio company shows users is usually the one he shipped alongside the founders. Before T3NK he built and shipped software end to end, which is why his diligence starts at the repository, not the cap table.

He writes the "decision notes" series on our [posts](/posts) page.
