Jamei designs how portfolio products actually feel — flows, interfaces, states and edge cases — and makes sure the design lands in the build, not just in Figma.

Her process starts with user research and ends with a shippable experience: she pairs directly with engineers on the components she specifies, so "the design" and "the product" are the same artifact by release time.

She keeps design debt as visible as technical debt, and has quietly killed more dark patterns inside portfolio apps than any audit.
