0xChat is a decentralized secure messaging app built on the Nostr protocol. It pairs private-key login with end-to-end encrypted DMs and contacts, encrypted private groups, and voice and video calls — with Lightning payments built in.

Your identity lives on Nostr, not on a platform account; conversations are encrypted client-side. 0xChat ships for [web](https://0xchat.com/) and Android, with the app source on [GitHub](https://github.com/0xchat-app).
