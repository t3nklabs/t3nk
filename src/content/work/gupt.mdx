GUPT runs entirely in your browser: your identity is a keypair derived on your device — no signup, no phone number, no email. Messages, calls, files and the built-in password, note and bookmark vaults are encrypted locally before they leave, and the relays that carry them only ever hold ciphertext. Voice, video and screen sharing flow peer-to-peer over WebRTC.

Self-hosting is first-class: run it from Docker, one-click on your [Umbrel home server](https://apps.umbrel.com/app/gupt), or install the native Linux app from Flathub (`flatpak install flathub com.besoeasy.gupt`). Source and issues live on [GitHub](https://github.com/besoeasy/gupt).
