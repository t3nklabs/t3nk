OnionHop routes your traffic through Tor with minimal ceremony: it can run as a local SOCKS proxy or a system-wide tunnel, and its **Smart Connect** engine auto-picks the connection strategy that survives your network — pre-testing and racing transports like obfs4, snowflake, webtunnel, conjure and DNS-tunnel modes, and remembering what worked. A bridge scanner tests and applies working bridges; a relay browser lets you pin entry and exit nodes; a CLI runs headless, even on a Raspberry Pi sharing Tor with your LAN.

Native builds for Windows, macOS and Linux; source, docs and issues live on [GitHub](https://github.com/center2055/OnionHop).
