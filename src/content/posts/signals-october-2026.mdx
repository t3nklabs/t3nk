**Self-hosting is a hiring signal, not just a cost signal.** Teams that run their own relays and relays-as-a-service both frame it as cost, but the teams that nail it are hiring differently — the relay admin and the SRE are now the same job.

**Nostr protocols matured twice this year.** The spec work is reaching the "boring, stable, documentable" phase — which is when real products get built on it (see 0xChat and GUPT in our WEB3 feed).

**CLI-first distribution is outgrowing dashboards.** The fastest-growing tools in our portfolio are installed with one command and configured in one file. The laptop flag: if your beta users run you in tmux, you're close.

**The pitch that opens with the fallback plan wins.** Show us what happens when the LLM vendor tripled prices and the incumbent shipped your top feature — the founders who prepared that slide are the ones who survive the bottom of the market.
