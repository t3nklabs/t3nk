It's the most common pitch of 2026: AI meeting summaries, but *this* one's different. We took one of those calls this month and passed. The transcript is not the moat.

**What they had.** A working product, real usage, a clean demo, and 40% week-over-week growth with zero CAC — classic seed-stage beautiful.

**Why we passed:**

1. **Routing costs.** Every feature was an LLM call, priced in someone else's margin. Growth made the problem bigger, not smaller.
2. **The incumbent is a keyboard shortcut.** Notion, Zoom and Slack didn't have to add this; they shipped it as a toggle.
3. **No code to join.** They wanted capital but not engineers on their repo. That's the opposite of what we're built to give.
4. **Nobody was deleting anything.** Retention was a cohort chart, not a habit.

**What would have changed our mind:** a wedge in a vertical where meetings *are* the product (hospitals, legal, displacement of an outsourced workflow), and a founder honest enough to show us the cost curve, not the growth curve.

Decision notes stay anonymous because signal matters more than ego.
