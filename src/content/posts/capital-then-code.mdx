Most venture writing is either a thesis deck with a skin or market research nobody asked for. This section is neither. It is the working margin of a fund that also writes code — notes from the treehouse between a term sheet and a pull request.

You will find three kinds of post here:

1. **Decision notes.** Why we passed, why we wired, and what we'd have needed to change our mind.
2. **Engineering log.** What our engineers are actually fixing inside portfolio repos — the boring, load-bearing stuff.
3. **Signals.** Short, dated observations from the edge of capital + code. No forecasts with a year attached.

The tone matches the site: plain type, no editorial flourishes, everything published when it's ready instead of on a schedule.
